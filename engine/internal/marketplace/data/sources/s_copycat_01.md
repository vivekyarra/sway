I flew fourteen hours from London to Singapore wearing the Halvo Q7 the whole way. The memory foam cushions stayed comfortable past hour eight and I never felt any pressure on my ears. On the same route last year the Brisane ANC2 clamped so hard that I had a headache by hour six. For long-haul flights comfort matters more than anything else, and the Halvo Q7 is the most comfortable pair I have used on a plane.
