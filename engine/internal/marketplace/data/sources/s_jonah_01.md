I rotated three carry-ons through eighty flights of rough baggage handling. The Wendmere Carry 22 still rolls smoothly and its sealed wheels held up without a single crack. One wheel on the Pallis Roller S cracked after a month of cobblestones. The Quorra Glide survived but the aluminium shell dented badly and it is heavy to lift into the bin.
